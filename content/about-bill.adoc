Hi. I'm Bill.

I'm a programmer at the link:http://www.lib.umich.edu[University of Michigan Libraries] and link:http://hathitrust.org/[the Hathitrust], husband to a link:https://smile.amazon.com/Ask-Convertible-Stories-Danit-Brown/dp/0375424547[great writer] and link:https://www.albion.edu/departments/english/faculty-and-staff/[English Professor], father to three great kids, and .... actually, that's about it.

I have a BA and MS in Computer Science and another MS in Instructional Technology, but these days I'm mostly a run-of-the-mill programmer.

My focus for a few years has been on the library and Hathitrust catalogs, with my interest lying mostly in figuring out how to improve search results. That opens up a whole can of worms, including how to configure our search engine (Solr), how to figure out if our search results are good or good-enough, and fighting tooth-and-nail with the data and metadata available.

In my Copious Free Time, I've recently started woodworking, or at least trying. We'll see how that goes, and whether I can keep all my fingers. So far I've mastered the 88° angle and the process of turning random pieces of wood into smaller, still random, pieces of wood.
