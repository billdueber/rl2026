Hi. I'm Bill.

I'm a programmer at the link:http://www.lib.umich.edu[University of Michigan
Libraries] (and, formerly, link:http://hathitrust.org/[the Hathitrust]),
husband
to a link:https://smile.amazon.com/Ask-Convertible-Stories-Danit-Brown/dp/0375424547[great writer] and link:https://www.albion.edu/departments/english/faculty-and-staff/[English Professor], father to three great kids, and .... actually, that's about it.

I have a BA and MS in Computer Science and another MS in Instructional
Technology, but these days I'm a run-of-the-mill programmer. My official
title probably has the word "enginneer" in it,
but I know some real engineers and I know what
I do doesn't deserve the title. Maybe some of the
rest of you are closer.

My focus for a few years has been on search
within the library, with my interest lying mostly in
figuring out how to improve search results. That opens up a whole can of
worms, including how to configure our search engine (Solr or OpenSearch), how
 to
figure out if our search results are good or good-enough, and fighting
tooth-and-nail with the data and metadata available. The advent of LLMs,
embeddings, and so on are making things more
complicated -- I'm still trying to decide if
they're materially better or not.

In my Copious Free Time, I've started woodworking, or at least trying. We'll
see how that goes, and whether I can keep all my fingers.
My specialty so far is taking a combination of hardwood,
softwood, and engineered products like MDF and
plywood and turning them into sawdust and garbage.
