Eleven Years…​

It’s been more than 11 years since I wrote a blog post.

That seems impossibly long ago now. The kids were 7, 5, and 2, and just trying to get to the end of the day felt like a struggle a lot of the time. I was going through a mid-life crisis — no red sports car, which I don’t know what I would have done with, anyway, but a lot of self-doubt and anxiety about whether I had completely lost any agency in my own life.

December 17, 2013 ·

A while back, Dreamhost had some problems and my blog and assorted other websites I help keep track of went down.

For more than two weeks.

Now, I understand that crap happens. And I understand that sometimes lots of things happen at once. But fundamentally, their infrastructure is such that they could lose everything on a machine and be unable to get it back for more than two weeks. I’m not a mathematician, but that’s not "five-nine" service.
