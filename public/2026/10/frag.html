Eleven Years…​

It’s been more than 11 years since I wrote a blog post.

That seems impossibly long ago now. The kids were 7, 5, and 2, and just trying to get to the end of the day felt like a struggle a lot of the time. I was going through a mid-life crisis — no red sports car, which I don’t know what I would have done with, anyway, but a lot of self-doubt and anxiety about whether I had completely lost any agency in my own life.
