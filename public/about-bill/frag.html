About Bill

Hi. I’m Bill.

I’m a programmer at the University of Michigan Libraries (and, formerly, the Hathitrust), husband to a great writer and English Professor, father to three great kids, and …​. actually, that’s about it.

I have a BA and MS in Computer Science and another MS in Instructional Technology, but these days I’m a run-of-the-mill programmer. My official title probably has the word "enginneer" in it, but I know some real engineers and I know what I do doesn’t deserve the title. Maybe some of the rest of you are closer.

My focus for a few years has been on search within the library, with my interest lying mostly in figuring out how to improve search results. That opens up a whole can of worms, including how to configure our search engine (Solr or OpenSearch), how to figure out if our search results are good or good-enough, and fighting tooth-and-nail with the data and metadata available. The advent of LLMs, embeddings, and so on are making things more complicated — I’m still trying to decide if they’re materially better or not.

With my Copious Free Time, I try to do some woodworking. My specialty so far is taking a combination of hardwood, softwood, and engineered products like MDF and plywood and using hand and power tools to turn them into sawdust and trash.
